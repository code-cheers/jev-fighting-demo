# Character and stage assets

Source: https://github.com/oosuhada/retro-king-of-fighters
Reference commit: 656732a0adc50a3bb17fae112283c4a8bcb86879
Paths: static/images/player/kyo, static/images/player/mai, static/images/background/0.gif.

The supplied GIF animation frames are packed into PNG atlases without redrawing the pixels. Animation timings are mapped to this demo's simplified rules. Block uses a held crouching pose plus a guard effect. Character art is presentation only: both sides retain identical move parameters. The stage image contains The King of Fighters 2001 branding.

SNK character/game artwork remains attributed to its original creators. Availability in a fan repository does not establish a license for commercial redistribution. This local experiment does not claim to be an official KOF game or a faithful recreation of its complete rules.
